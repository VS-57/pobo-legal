Pobo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Pobo: Jump & Run Adventure (“Pobo”, “we”, “the app”) is a game made for children. We built it so that it collects nothing about the people who play it.

Summary

Pobo does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no network features. The app works fully offline.

Information stored on the device

To remember progress, Pobo saves a small file on the device with unlocked levels, stars earned, coins, chosen character colour and sound settings. This file never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

No tracking, no third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and it does not track anyone across other apps or websites.

Children

Pobo is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). There are no links out of the app, no chat and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
