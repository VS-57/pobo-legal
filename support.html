Pobo — Support

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Thanks for playing Pobo! If something isn't working, or you have an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact

Email info@enucuzsistem.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Use the left and right arrow buttons to run and the big yellow button to jump. Jump on critters to squish them, collect coins and find the three stars in each level.

How do I unlock more levels? Finish a level to open the next one. Swipe between worlds with the blue arrows on the level screen.

How do I get new colours for Pobo? Collect coins in levels, then tap the character button on the main screen and choose a colour.

There's no sound. Check the speaker and music buttons in the pause menu or in settings (the gear on the main screen), and your device volume.

Does Pobo need the internet? No. Pobo works fully offline and has no ads or purchases.
